Research

Control theory gives us the language of feedback, robustness and guarantees. We bring that language to learning, to robots and to AI systems, so that they are not only capable but provably safe, secure and trustworthy.

Safe Physical AI

Large language and vision models now plan and act for robots. We study how these systems can be manipulated into harmful physical actions — RoboPAIR showed that LLM-controlled robots can be jailbroken — and build guardrails such as RoboGuard that keep robot behavior safe in context.

Trustworthy AI

Jailbreak attacks and defenses for language models — PAIR, SmoothLLM and JailbreakBench — safety verification of neural networks, distribution-free uncertainty quantification with conformal prediction, and principled ways to verify, oversee and control the AI agents built on these models.

Layered Control

The theory of layered control architectures: hierarchical abstractions and bisimulation that let simple models stand in for complex systems with guarantees, and layered multirate control that couples slow planning with fast feedback.

Formal Methods for Safe Autonomy

Guarantees for autonomous systems: temporal logic specifications and their quantitative robustness, reactive mission and motion planning, verification of systems with neural network controllers, and planning and control with conformal prediction when the environment is uncertain or unknown.
